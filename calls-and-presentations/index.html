Keep your Mac awake during calls and presentations

Why your screen dims halfway through a call, and how to make it stop for good.

Why it happens

Your Mac decides you've gone away when nobody touches the keyboard or trackpad for a while. During a long call, a webinar or a slideshow you're watching, not typing, so the screen dims, the screen saver starts, or the Mac locks in the middle of a meeting.

Make it automatic with Hootle

Hootle can wake up on its own whenever a call or presentation app is open, and go back to sleep when it quits:

  1. Get Hootle from the Mac App Store (it's free).
  2. Right-click the owl in the menu bar and choose Settings….
  3. Under Wake Up for Apps, click Add App… and pick Zoom, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, Webex, Keynote or PowerPoint.

That's it. While any of those apps is open, your Mac stays awake and won't lock. During calls the owl even puts on a little headset. Hootle never shows "Anyone there?" while an app keeps it awake, so it won't interrupt a presentation.

Just this once?

Click the owl to turn it on, or right-click it for a timer: 15 minutes to 4 hours, any length, or until the end of the meeting. The time left shows next to the owl.
