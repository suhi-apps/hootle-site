Hootle vs Amphetamine

Amphetamine is the best-known keep-awake app for the Mac, and a great one. Hootle takes a simpler, friendlier approach. Here's how they compare.

Choose Amphetamine if…

you need to keep a MacBook awake with the lid closed, or you want dozens of fine-grained triggers (IP address, USB devices, downloads) and don't mind a more complex setup.

Choose Hootle if…

you want one click on, one click off, and an app that turns itself off when the job is done: when your call app quits, the timer ends, the battery runs low or you've walked away. And if you'd like a little owl to keep you company.
